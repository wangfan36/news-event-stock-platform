In practical terms:

- Personal research, study, experiments, testing, and hobby projects are allowed when there is no anticipated commercial application.
- The license also permits the noncommercial organizations specifically listed in its `Noncommercial Organizations` section.
- Commercial use is not granted. Using News Alpha in a paid product or service, SaaS offering, client deliverable, monetized workflow, or internal business operation requires a separate written license from the copyright holder.
- Redistribution and modification are allowed only for purposes permitted by the license, and recipients must receive the license terms and required notices.
- Third-party packages, data, feeds, trademarks, and other materials remain subject to their own terms. This license covers only rights the News Alpha licensor can grant.

The examples above are a plain-language summary, not replacement legal terms. The exact text in [LICENSE](LICENSE) controls. If a planned use is unclear, treat it as not licensed until the copyright holder gives written permission. Commercial-license enquiries can be opened privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.

## Why This License

The goal is to allow personal, educational, and other qualifying noncommercial use while reserving commercial exploitation. PolyForm provides a standard, publicly versioned software license for that policy. Because it restricts fields of use, it does not satisfy the OSI Open Source Definition, which requires permission for commercial use. The project therefore uses the precise term **source-available**.

Other common choices were not used:

- Creative Commons recommends against using its licenses for software.
- Commons Clause mainly restricts selling the software itself and can still permit other commercial use, so it does not match a general noncommercial policy.
- Some source-available projects use the same dual-path wording: free noncommercial use under PolyForm, with a separate written commercial agreement where offered.

## Legal Context and References

This licensing approach is based on copyright ownership and contractual permission, not on the repository merely being public:

- The [Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China](https://www.npc.gov.cn/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html) lists rights including reproduction, distribution, information-network dissemination, and adaptation, and permits copyright owners to license those rights.
- Articles 8 and 18 of the [Regulations on Computers Software Protection](https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=914) describe software copyright rights and written licensing of their exercise.
- The [Supreme People's Court Intellectual Property Court](https://ipc.court.gov.cn/zh-cn/news/view-842.html) explains that open-source license terms may operate as a public offer accepted through use of the software, with compliance and termination consequences determined by those terms.
- [GitHub's licensing guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository) explains that making a repository public does not by itself grant general reuse rights beyond GitHub's platform terms.
- The [OSI Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd) does not allow discrimination against fields of endeavor, including commercial use.
- The authoritative license text is published by the [PolyForm Project](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0/).
- The [Creative Commons FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/) recommends using a software-specific license rather than a CC license for software.
- The [Commons Clause](https://commonsclause.com/) illustrates a narrower restriction focused on selling the software.
- Public descriptions from [Multigent](https://github.com/multigent/multigent/blob/main/LICENSE), [DCTL](https://www.dctl.sh/pricing), and [Trueform](https://trueform.polydera.com/py/about/license) show how other projects distinguish noncommercial and separately licensed commercial use.
